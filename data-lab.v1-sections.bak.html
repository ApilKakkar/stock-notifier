Quarterly fundamentals

P/E PE_RATIO

Price ÷ earnings per share. How many rupees/dollars you pay for ₹1/$1 of profit. Low = cheap (or troubled).

P/B PX_TO_BOOK_RATIO

Price ÷ book value per share. Valuation vs net assets; most useful for banks/asset-heavy firms.

EV/EBITDA CURRENT_EV_TO_T12M_EBITDA

Enterprise value ÷ operating cash profit. Capital-structure-neutral valuation; compares firms with different debt.

ROE RETURN_COM_EQY

Net income ÷ shareholders’ equity. How efficiently the company turns owners’ money into profit — a quality signal.

Debt/Equity TOT_DEBT_TO_TOT_EQY

Total debt ÷ equity. Leverage and balance-sheet risk; high values hurt in rate-hike regimes.

EPS IS_EPS

Earnings per share. Base for growth and earnings-surprise features.

Revenue & growth SALES_REV_TURN

Top-line sales and YoY/QoQ growth. Growth factor; harder to manipulate than earnings.

Gross margin GROSS_MARGIN

(Revenue − COGS) ÷ revenue. Pricing power; stable margins = quality.

Consensus EPS BEST_EPS

Analyst consensus estimate. Actual − consensus = earnings surprise, a classic drift signal.

Sector GICS_SECTOR_NAME

Industry classification. Sector-neutral ranking; avoid accidentally betting on one sector.

Point-in-time rule: join fundamentals on the filing/announcement date, not period-end. If only period-end is known, lag 45–60 days. Otherwise: look-ahead bias.

Corporate actions

Splits & bonuses

Change share count without changing value. Must adjust prices or returns look like crashes.

Dividends

Cash paid out; price drops on ex-date. Captured by total-return index; track yield separately.

Mergers, demergers, delistings

Ticker changes and exits. Critical for survivorship-free history. Bloomberg CACS.

Buybacks / rights issues

Shift share count and signal management view on valuation.

Experiments & principles

Satellite allocation first Decided

Core stays in index funds; model runs on paper, then 5–10% satellite. Compare vs index for 1–2 years before trusting.

Exp 1 · 12-1 momentum Idea

Top-10 NIFTY 50 by 12-month return (skip last month), monthly rebalance vs buy-and-hold.

Exp 2 · Quality + value Idea

Rank on ROE, D/E, EV/EBITDA; sector-neutral; quarterly rebalance.

Exp 3 · Low-volatility Idea

Lowest 1-year volatility names — target similar return with smaller drawdowns.

Exp 4 · Regime switch Idea

De-risk to gold/cash when India VIX spikes or FII outflows persist.

Exp 5 · India + US mix in INR Idea

Does US diversification beat NIFTY after FX spread, LRS lag, TCS and tax?

Tax-aware rebalancing Principle

India equity: STCG 20% (<12m), LTCG 12.5% above ₹1.25L. Foreign stocks: slab rate <24m, 12.5% after. Prefer holding winners past thresholds. Verify with a CA.

Explainability is key Principle

Every signal ships with a plain-English rationale (top feature contributions via SHAP + LLM summary). No black-box trades.

Honest validation Principle

Walk-forward only; one-day execution lag; costs + taxes + FX. Red flags: CAGR >25%, Sharpe >2, smooth curve.
